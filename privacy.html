Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 28, 2026

Data Collection

MouseEverywhere does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app operates entirely on your device.

Preferences

Your settings (such as keyboard shortcuts and skin preferences) are stored locally on your device using macOS UserDefaults. They are never sent off your device.

Analytics

MouseEverywhere does not include any analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs. We have no way of knowing how you use the app unless you tell us directly.

Third Parties

No third-party services, libraries, or APIs are used in connection with your data.
