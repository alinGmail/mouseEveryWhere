# MouseEverywhere

**A macOS menu bar app that mirrors your cursor across multiple screens.**

## Core Features

### 1. Cursor Ghost Mirroring
A translucent ghost cursor appears on every screen *except* the one your real mouse is on. Each ghost tracks the same proportional position — so when your cursor is at the top-left of one screen, ghosts appear at the top-left of all others. You always know exactly where your cursor will land before you even move it.

### 2. Instant Screen Jumping
Forget dragging your mouse across monitor bezels. Hit **Ctrl+D** to teleport your cursor to the next screen, or **Ctrl+F** to jump to the previous one, preserving your relative position. Shortcuts are fully rebindable.

### 3. The Moon Skin
Switch to a playful moon cursor skin and a tiny rocket appears, chasing the moon across non-active screens. It flies in from the outside, then smoothly locks into orbit around the ghost cursor using physics-based steering.

## Why?

Multi-monitor setups are great, but dragging your mouse across bezels to reach another screen is slow and you often lose visual track of where you are. Mouse Everywhere fixes both problems: you **see** ghost cursors on all screens so you know your position, and you **jump** to them with a keystroke.

### More reasons to love it

- Helpful for ultra-wide monitors too, not just multi-screen setups
- Cursor is always visible on all screens
- Reduces physical mouse movement
- Minimal resource usage
